Straight answers

The questions we get asked

Does solar work in Scotland?+

Yes. Panels run on daylight, not heat — Scottish daylight hours across the year are ample. Your survey models the actual yield for your roof pitch, orientation and shading before you commit.

How long does an install take?+

Most domestic solar and battery installations are completed within a few days on site. Heat pumps take longer where heat emitters need changing. You'll have a dated schedule before we start.

Are you accredited?+

We're MCS certified and TrustMark endorsed, which is also what unlocks eligibility for the main grant and export schemes.

Do you subcontract the work?+

No. The team that surveys your property designs it, installs it and handles the aftercare — so nothing gets lost between trades.

What happens after handover?+

We walk you through monitoring your output, register your warranties, and stay on the end of the phone for maintenance and advice.
